## Mechanics 🔩

![Screenshot](https://i.ibb.co/QjnkGGBZ/orig.jpg)
As befits any fighting game, here you have to beat your opponent! 🔥

There are _three_ attacks in your robot's equipment - __upper__, __lower__, and a __cumulative super attack__. You can build your _battle build_ by buying new techniques in the store and __combining__ them. 🤜🏻

Initially, the robot is weak and slow, after each level a certain number of _skill points_ are given, so you make your bot more __agile, stronger, and more resilient__. 💪🏻

![Gameplay](https://i.ibb.co/CKdV52f9/Robot-Gameplay.gif)

Every fifth level is a _boss fight_, bosses differ in their behavior from ordinary enemies and have __special techniques__. 🤖

The game is not just about keystrokes or mindless button taps. Here you will have to __develop your own strategy to win.__ 🧠
